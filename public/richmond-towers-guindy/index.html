About VGN Projects Estates

When VGN was founded in 1942 as a brick manufacturing unit by Mr. Gurusamy, it was the start of an incredible legacy. He started a brick kiln factory, manufactured bricks in the name of VGN, and acquired large parcels of land to manufacture bricks at various locations in Chennai. He worked hard to build goodwill and earned a name as a reputed brick manufacturer in Chennai. His grandson Mr. Pratish Vedhappudi, Managing Director of VGN Projects Estates, studied Construction Engineering at Cambridge University, England, UK.
He entered the real estate business in the year 2004. His eye for detail, engineering prowess, and business acumen have resulted in the construction of large residential projects in and around Chennai. Since 2008, he has been developing large residential townships in various parts of Chennai. He acquired many prime lands in the city, to cater to the evolving needs of home buyers, and provided customers with their dream homes. A few recent multi-storeyed landmark residential projects such as VGN Notting Hill Nungambakkam, VGN Fairmont at Guindy, VGN Coasta at East Coast Road (ECR) and other notable projects at Ambattur.
